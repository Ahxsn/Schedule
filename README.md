## 📱 How to Use It on Your Mobile Phone (100% Free)

You have two instant options to have this on your phone:

### Option A: Free GitHub Pages (Recommended — Takes 2 Minutes)
1. Create a free repository on [GitHub](https://github.com).
2. Upload the files in this folder (`index.html`, `app.js`, `manifest.json`, `sw.js`, `icon.svg`).
3. Go to **Settings ➔ Pages** ➔ select **main branch** ➔ click **Save**.
4. GitHub gives you a free live URL (e.g. `https://yourname.github.io/empire-os`).
5. Open that link on your phone (Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android):
   - **On iPhone:** Tap the Share button ➔ tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
   - **On Android:** Tap the 3 dots ➔ tap **"Add to Home screen / Install App"**.
6. It now acts like a native mobile app with offline caching and audio alerts!

### Option B: Syncing Data Between Laptop & Mobile
* In Empire OS, click the **Gear (Settings)** icon in the top right.
* Click **"Export Backup (.json)"** to download your progress file.
* Send it to your phone (via WhatsApp/Email/Telegram) and click **"Import Backup"** in Empire OS on mobile. Your XP, Chai aur Code progress, and checkpoints stay 100% in sync!
